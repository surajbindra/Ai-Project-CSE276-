ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Smarter decisions for better campus events.

CampusPlan is an academic prototype exploring how artificial intelligence techniques can support university event scheduling and venue allocation.

It combines constraint-based filtering, search-state exploration and penalty-based ranking. A policy assistant prototype is also included to demonstrate document-grounded assistance.
